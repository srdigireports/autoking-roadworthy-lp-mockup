What is a roadworthy certificate and when do I need one? +
A roadworthy certificate (RWC) confirms your vehicle meets QLD minimum safety standards. You need one when selling a registered vehicle, re-registering an unregistered vehicle, or clearing a defect notice. Our QLD-licensed inspectors assess brakes, tyres, lights, steering, and structural components.
How long does the inspection take? +
Most roadworthy inspections take between 45 and 90 minutes depending on the vehicle. You do not need to be present for the entire inspection — our inspector will contact you when complete and walk you through the results in person.
Where exactly do you come to? +
We come to your home, workplace, carpark or any accessible location across your area. As long as there is enough space for our inspector to safely carry out the inspection, we can come to you. No workshop visit required.
How much does a roadworthy certificate cost? +
Roadworthy certificates start from $130 and vary by vehicle make, model, and age. Your exact price is confirmed before the inspector arrives — no surprises on the day. We give you a firm quote when you book.
Do I get the certificate on the same day? +
Yes. If your vehicle passes, the certificate is issued the same day. If minor items need attention, your inspector will explain exactly what needs to be fixed before the certificate can be issued.
